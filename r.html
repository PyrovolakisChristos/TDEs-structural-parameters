📋 Column Definitions

Galaxy Name: TDE event designation
Fit Model: Structural model used (Sersic, Sersic+exp, Sersic+psf, etc.)
Re: Bulge effective radius (kpc)
Rs: Disk scale length (kpc)
ibulge, idisk: Inclination angles (degrees)
qbulge, qdisk: Axis ratios
z: Redshift
Magnitude: Total magnitude in the respective band
n (Sersic): Sersic index (n=1: exponential, n=4: de Vaucouleurs)
Class: Spectral classification (H: Hydrogen, He: Helium)
